# Plan: Sound

**Status:** active
**Goal:** After "Click/Tap to enter" the night has sound. The altar fire, the torches and the
pillar of fire crackle and roar from where they stand, the wind moves across the desert and
rises and falls with the cloth, and crickets sing out on the sand. Inside the tent the outside
is muffled to near silence. A speaker button (and `M` on desktop) mutes it. Frame time, draw
calls and load time stay as they are on desktop and on phones.

**In scope:**
- A Web Audio engine (`src/audio/`): context unlocked by the enter click/tap, a listener that
  follows the camera, buses (fire, wind, insects), mute toggle, pause when the tab is hidden.
- Recorded CC0 samples for every sound: fire loops and single crackles, a wind bed, night
  insects. Positional fires; wind and insects as stereo beds.
- Muffling inside the tent (Holy Place, more in the Holy of Holies).
- A phone memory and load check with the production build.

**Out of scope:** footsteps, cloth flutter, camp sounds (livestock, voices), music, any sound
for the menorah, incense or laver, analytics events for mute, a volume slider.

**Assumptions** (move to `docs/DECISIONS.md` as steps land):
- **All sounds are recorded CC0 samples** (Kyle's choice). Kyle auditions the candidates and
  approves each download: agents can't listen, and downloads need his OK.
- **Sound is on by default** after the enter click/tap, with a mute button; the mute state is
  remembered in `localStorage`. `?audio=0` turns sound off entirely (no fetch, no context).
- **No effect on load time:** no audio is fetched before the loading screen goes. The files are
  fetched (not decoded) on idle after `scene_loaded`; the `AudioContext` is created and the
  files decoded inside the enter gesture, and everything fades in over ~1.5 s. Nothing creates
  an `AudioContext` before a user gesture (that logs a console warning).
- **Format: AAC in `.m4a`.** It's the one format every target decodes, iPhone Safari included.
  AAC adds encoder priming silence, so loops play with `loopStart`/`loopEnd` taken from a
  manifest, not by looping the whole buffer.
- **Budgets:** download ≤ 1.5 MB in all; decoded PCM ≤ 25 MB in all (float32: 48 kHz mono is
  ~11.5 MB per minute). Fire loops mono and short (≤ 10 s); wind and insects stereo ≤ 30 s.
- **Panning:** `equalpower` in the mobile tier, `HRTF` in `high`, as a new `QUALITY` knob
  `audioHrtf`. Main-thread cost per frame (listener and a few gains) under 0.05 ms.
- **The pillar of fire** has a deep, slow roar from above (Num 9:15-16 gives no sound; artistic
  license, like its size). It can reuse a fire recording pitched down and low-passed.
- **Inside the tent** (the same test as `fx.setInside`), every outside bus goes through a
  low-pass (~700 Hz) and drops ~12 dB, insects are silent; the Holy of Holies drops a further
  ~6 dB. No sound inside of its own: the lamps and incense are near silent.
- ffmpeg is needed to trim, loop and encode. It isn't installed: Step 1 asks Kyle to run
  `brew install ffmpeg`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Source and prepare the CC0 recordings** — model: Sonnet / effort: high
  **Goal:** Find CC0 recordings for: a crackling wood fire (close, for the altar), small torch
  fires, single crackles and pops (6–10), a deep fire roar (pillar, or derive it from the altar
  fire), desert night wind (soft, no whistling through objects, no rain or birds), and night
  insects (crickets, sparse, no frogs or birds). Search CC0-only sources (Freesound with the
  CC0 filter, OpenGameArt CC0, Sonniss is NOT CC0). For each sound, give Kyle a shortlist of 2–3
  candidates with the page link, licence and duration, and **stop for him to listen and pick**
  and to approve each download. Then, with ffmpeg: trim, convert fires to mono, make seamless
  loops (crossfade the seam), normalize loudness (fire loops and wind about −23 LUFS, single
  crackles peak −3 dBFS), encode AAC `.m4a` (fires 64 kbps mono, beds 96–128 kbps stereo), and
  write `public/assets/audio/manifest.json` with each file's loop points in seconds (allowing
  for AAC priming) and its decoded size. Create `CREDITS.md` with title, author, source URL and
  licence for each file.

  `CREDITS.md` (new). Keep raw downloads out of the repo (scratchpad).
