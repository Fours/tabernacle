# Plan: Fire

**Status:** active
**Goal:** The altar and torch flames look and move like real fire, through a new way of
rendering the flame body, while their sparks stay exactly as they are. The pillar of fire's
swirling embers become many small motes, and its flames read at the scale and character of the
altar's fire instead of a few huge billows.

**In scope / out of scope:**
- In: the flame body of `createFire` (altar + torches), its light flicker sync, the pillar of
  fire's embers and the look of its flame column, presets, DECISIONS, the performance baseline.
- Out: sparks, embers, ash and smoke of `createFire` (`fireParticles.js` stays untouched), the
  menorah's lamp flames (`createLampFlames`), the incense smoke, the pillar's shaft of light, its
  position and overall size, light counts and shadow casters.

**Assumptions:**
- "Different approach" = replace the camera-facing flame sheets with a raymarched volumetric
  flame (a small bounding volume per fire, 3D noise advected upward, temperature → the shared
  blackbody ramp). The builder may choose a hybrid (e.g. volume body + a few fine sheet tongues
  at the tips) if a pure volume can't get crisp tongue edges; the result must not look like the
  old sheets.
- The pillar of fire keeps its column size and position (Num 9:15–16 decisions in DECISIONS
  "Interpretation"); "flames too big" means its tongues and billows are too coarse, so the
  column gets many smaller, altar-like tongues on its surface rather than shrinking.
- Graphics before frames: each fire step may spend up to ~+1.5 ms at its main preset
  (throttled mode) and +0–2 draws, but must try the obvious optimizations (tight bounds,
  early-out on empty space, step count by screen size/distance, half-res only if it looks the
  same).
- Sparks are judged unchanged if `fireParticles.js` is not edited and every option it reads
  (`radius`, `height`, spark/ember counts, `sparkSize`, seeds) keeps its value at each call site.

## Steps

- [x] **Step 1: Volumetric flame body for the altar** — model: Opus / effort: medium (risky:
  new shader technique with heavy visual judgment; re-run at high if the first pass falls short)
  **Goal:** Replace the flame sheets in `createFlames` (used by `createFire`) with a new flame
  renderer that behaves like real fire:
  - A raymarched volume inside a tight box/cylinder around each fire (one draw per fire, like
    now). Fuel-driven: dense and hot at the bed, tongues that detach, rise, accelerate and
    break off into flamelets that burn out (not a fixed silhouette with scrolling noise).
  - Turbulence that is fine and fast low, larger and slower higher; tongues stretch upward
    (buoyancy), lean with the per-fire wind, and pulse with the fire's `level` so the light
    flicker and the visible flame agree.
  - Colour by temperature through `FIRE_RAMP_GLSL`: blue-ish/transparent at the very base of
    each tongue is optional, then a bright yellow-orange body, orange-red thin tips, soft
    translucent edges, no hard white blob; HDR core reaches bloom.
  - Looks right from the side, above (`altartop`) and close (`fire`), and the grate bars still
    cut the flames' bases.
  - Keep `createFire`'s API (options and return value); add options only if needed. The old
    sheet code is deleted, not left behind a flag. Keep `fireFlames.js` under the line limit
    (split into e.g. `fireVolume.js` if needed; update the AGENTS.md code map).
  - Tune the altar first. Torches will use the same code; leave them looking reasonable,
    their tuning is Step 2.
  **Files:** `src/scene/fireFlames.js`, `src/scene/fire.js`, `src/scene/fireCommon.js`,
  `src/scene/altarFire.js`; read `src/scene/pillarOfFire.js` for the existing 3D noise texture
  and raymarch pattern (reuse or share it via `fireCommon.js` if it fits).
  **Spec:** AGENTS.md "Three.js r187 gotchas" (fog chunks, NaN/pow), "Light budget", "Fog";
  DECISIONS "Fire & lighting" (flame sheets entry is superseded; rewrite it).
  **Accept when:**
