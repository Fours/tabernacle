# Plan: Tabernacle interior

**Status:** active
**Goal:** The player walks through the east screen into the Holy Place and sees it lit by the
menorah: gold walls, the cherubim-woven linen ceiling, the table of showbread on the north, the
menorah on the south, and the incense altar before the veil. The veil blocks the Holy of
Holies; the ark with its cherubim is seen only through free-camera presets.

**In scope:**
- Walk-in entrance; interior collision.
- Inner linen covering seen as the ceiling, with woven cherubim.
- Veil with its 4 pillars, silver sockets and gold hooks.
- Menorah (curved branches) with 7 lit lamps; interior lighting.
- Table of showbread with loaves, frankincense and utensils.
- Altar of incense with a faint incense smoke.
- Ark, mercy seat and two cherubim as winged human figures.

**Out of scope:** walking into the Holy of Holies; priests or people; the anointing oil,
laver interaction or any ritual animation; changes to the exterior beyond what the entrance
needs.

**Assumptions** (move each to `docs/DECISIONS.md` when its step lands):
- Placement (Ex 26:35; 40:22–26): table on the north side of the Holy Place, menorah on the
  south, both about 2/5 of the way in from the entrance (x ≈ −8 cubits, z ≈ ∓2.5); incense
  altar centered just east of the veil (x ≈ −18.5). Ark centered in the Holy of Holies
  (x = −25, z = 0).
- The veil hangs at x = −20 (under the linen clasps, Ex 26:33), its pillars on the Holy of
  Holies side, hooks on their east face. The 4 pillars are evenly spaced across the 10-cubit
  width.
- The inner linen covering is visible only as the ceiling; its sides hang down the outside of
  the boards under the goat hair and stay unmodelled (rabbinic: the boards show gold inside).
- Cherubim on textiles (ceiling and veil) are woven figures in blue, purple and scarlet on
  linen, in a procedural texture.
- Ark cherubim are **kneeling winged human figures with childlike faces** (rabbinic), facing
  each other and looking down at the mercy seat, wings raised and spread forward to meet
  above it. Mercy seat 1 handbreadth thick.
- Ark long axis N–S; poles run E–W and stay in the rings, ends pointing toward the veil
  (1 Kgs 8:8).
- Menorah: curved (semicircular) branches, ~3 cubits tall (18 handbreadths), on a tripod base;
  lamps are small clay-shaped gold bowls facing the center.
- Showbread: 12 loaves in two stacks of six with a gold frankincense cup on each stack.
- Lighting: the menorah adds **one** non-shadow point light (11 point lights total); its 7
  flames are emissive (bloom) plus the existing small fire sprites if cheap. The Holy of
  Holies gets no light source: the ark reads only through env-map reflections and the menorah
  spill over/around the veil. Never toggle `light.visible` at runtime (changes the light count
  and recompiles every shader); use intensity instead if gating is needed.
- Budgets for interior views: ≤ 130 draw calls and ≤ 6.5 ms per frame (measured as in
  AGENTS.md). Exterior views must stay within the current baseline +5 draw calls.
