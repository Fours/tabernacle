# Spec: what the text says

This is the biblical reference for every object in the scene. Where the text is silent, the
default given here applies unless `docs/DECISIONS.md` records a different choice. DECISIONS.md
wins, because it records what was actually built.

All measurements are in cubits (`CUBIT = 0.45 m`). East = +X, North = −Z, and the origin is
the center of the courtyard.

## Courtyard (Ex 27:9–19; 38:9–20)

- The enclosure is **100 cubits** east–west by **50 cubits** north–south.
- The hangings are fine twined linen (white), **5 cubits high**.
- There are **60 pillars** in total: 20 on each long side and 10 on each short side.
  - Pillars stand on **bronze sockets** (bases).
  - Each has **silver hooks**, **silver fillets** (bands or rods connecting the pillars) and a
    **silver-overlaid capital**.
  - The hangings are held with **bronze pegs and cords**.
  - The text doesn't give the pillars' size.
- On the east side, the **20-cubit gate screen** is in the center. It is blue, purple, scarlet
  and fine twined linen, embroidered, 5 cubits high, on 4 pillars. On each side of it is a
  **15-cubit hanging** on 3 pillars.

## Tabernacle tent (Ex 26; 36:8–38)

- Interior: **30 cubits long (E–W) × 10 wide × 10 high**. The Holy Place is the east
  20 cubits; the Holy of Holies is the west 10.
- The walls are acacia **boards 10 cubits tall and 1.5 wide**, overlaid with gold.
  - There are 20 boards on the south, 20 on the north, and 6 on the west plus 2 corner boards.
  - Each board stands on **2 silver sockets**.
  - Each side has **5 bars**, and the middle bar runs the full length.
  - The text doesn't give the boards' thickness.
- The east entrance is a **screen** of blue, purple, scarlet and linen, embroidered, hung on
  **5 acacia pillars overlaid with gold**, standing on **bronze sockets**.
- The coverings, from the inside out:
  1. **Linen curtains with cherubim woven in**: 10 curtains of 28×4 cubits, joined into a
     28×40 sheet with loops and **gold clasps**.
  2. **Goat-hair curtains** (dark): 11 curtains of 30×4, making 30×44, with **bronze clasps**.
     They hang to about 1 cubit above the ground on the sides (Ex 26:13). The sixth curtain is
     doubled at the front, and the extra half curtain hangs over the back.
  3. **Ram skins dyed red.** The text gives no dimensions.
  4. **Tachash skins** on the outside. The animal and the color are uncertain.
- Interior furnishings (veil, ark, menorah, table, incense altar) are not built yet.

## Bronze altar (Ex 27:1–8; 38:1–7)

- **5×5 cubits, 3 high**, hollow, made of acacia boards overlaid with **bronze**.
- **Horns** on the four corners, of one piece with the altar.
- A **ledge** (*karkov*) runs around it. Under it is a **bronze network grating** reaching
  halfway up the altar.
- **4 bronze rings** sit at the grating's corners. **Two acacia poles overlaid with bronze**
  pass through the rings.
- Its utensils are pots for the ashes, shovels, basins, forks and firepans.
- A perpetual fire burns on it (Lev 6:13).

## Laver (Ex 30:17–21; 38:8)

- Made of bronze, on a bronze base, between the tent and the altar. The text gives no
  dimensions. It was made from the mirrors of the women who served at the entrance.

## Placement (traditional two-squares layout)

- **Tent** in the western 50×50 square. Its interior spans x = −30 … 0, so its back wall is
  20 cubits from the west hanging. It is centered at z = 0.
- **Altar** in the center of the eastern square (x = +25, z = 0).
- **Laver** between them, slightly south (about x = +10, z = +3).

## World

- The ground is desert sand and gravel in the wilderness.
- It is night. The main light is the warm firelight from the altar, with a dim, cool fill
  from the moon and stars.
- The torches are artistic license and must look period-appropriate: wooden posts with
  pitch-soaked wrapped heads, or simple bronze bowls. Nothing modern.
